# Roadmap and open gates

The roadmap expresses intended engineering work, not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Gate 1 — Controlled Devnet validation

- Qualify a release build and verify program identity / chain configuration.
- Complete key custody, offline backup and separation of deployment authorities.
- Execute approved, traceable Devnet tests with independently checked results.
- Document source-to-build hashes, negative tests and operational recovery evidence.

## Gate 2 — Stronger trust and privacy assurance

- Select and validate authoritative credential issuers, revocation/status sources and trust anchors.
- Demonstrate third-party interoperability of the supported credential/presentation flow.
- Calibrate liveness, capture integrity, face-match and fraud-resistance behavior with appropriate testing.
- Review privacy, logging, retention, consent and applicable regulatory obligations.

## Gate 3 — Partner runtime and independent security review

- Complete controlled partner capacity, settlement, signed results and operational monitoring.
- Independently review Solana program, prover/verifier, relayer and key management; remediate findings.
- Publish supported SDK and integration documentation where disclosure is safe.
- Separate production activation from prototype milestones through explicit release authorization.

## Technical roadmap / optional research

Longer-term work may include recursive zero-knowledge proof aggregation, stronger local proof-generation isolation with TEEs, advanced photonic/depth sensing such as LiDAR, pupillary reflex and micro-expression research, multi-device verification, stronger decentralized trust assumptions and future multichain adapters.

**These are research or roadmap activities, not live features.** Technical feasibility, privacy impact, accessibility, cost and adversarial robustness require independent evaluation before product inclusion.
